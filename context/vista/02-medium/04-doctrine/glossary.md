# App Doctrine — Glossary

Terms owned by the doctrine contract. Cross-cutting leitwörter (`app`, `template`,
`doctrine`) live in the [root ontology](../../ontology.md); the per-kind template
bodies live in [06-templates](../06-templates/glossary.md). Glossary is inherited
downward (decision 0067).

## Ask

The BLUF section of an [app](../../ontology.md#app): what it needs from the reviewer —
recommendations awaiting sign-off and open questions needing a verdict, each with a
one-line lean and a link to its evidence. An app with no ask must be explicitly
declared informational. The `ask` doctrine block renders it; leading with the ask is
the global doctrine default (MED.DOC-R32).
