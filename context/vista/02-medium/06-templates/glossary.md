# Templates — Glossary

Terms owned by the templates node. The `template` leitwort itself lives in the
[root ontology](../../ontology.md); the `archetype → template` rename note is in the
[doctrine glossary](../04-doctrine/glossary.md). Glossary is inherited downward
(decision 0067).

## Template kind

The kind of an [app](../../ontology.md#app), named by which
[template](../../ontology.md#template) it was instantiated from: **review** (architecture
/ design), **design-interview**, **options-comparison**, **plan/sign-off**,
**telemetry-exploration**, **code-recap**. Agent-declared (decision 0014); it selects
the body doctrine the template realizes (see [spec.md](./spec.md)). An app may be a
**hybrid** that composes more than one kind's body under one [ask](../04-doctrine/glossary.md#ask).
